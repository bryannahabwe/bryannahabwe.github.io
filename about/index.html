About

Nahabwe Brian

Senior Backend Engineer specializing in Java, Spring Boot, Payments, and Financial Systems based in Kampala, Uganda.

Nahabwe Brian (also written Brian Nahabwe) is a Senior Backend Engineer specializing in Java, Spring Boot, Payments, and Financial Systems based in Kampala, Uganda, with 8+ years building payment, investment and savings platforms. He is currently a Software Engineer at XENO Investment, a digital fund manager, where he built the MTN and Airtel mobile-money integrations that now carry more than 90% of client deposits, and the USSD channel that lets clients on basic phones invest.

Before XENO he spent nearly five years at OMNI-Tech building data-collection, workflow and reporting systems for NGOs and development partners. Alongside his full-time role he has built payment services connecting banks and mobile networks for Rexial, delivered the events platform for the African Union's AU INNOFEST '26 as sole developer, and co-founded Moozo, a multi-tenant school management platform.

His work centres on the design calls behind features: how a system is split up, how tenants are kept apart, how services communicate, where it runs and how it is observed. He works mainly in Java, Spring Boot, Groovy/Grails, Python and Kafka, with PostgreSQL, MySQL and Redis, on Docker, Kubernetes, GCP and AWS, and secures systems with Keycloak, OAuth2 and RBAC.

He holds a BSc in Software Engineering from Makerere University and completed the McKinsey Forward Programme. He speaks English, Runyankole and Luganda.
