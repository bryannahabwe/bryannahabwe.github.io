How I Build

The shape of the payment systems I build.

  1. 01 Request. A customer app or USSD session reaches the API gateway — OAuth2 and rate limits first.
  2. 02 Route. The payments service assigns an idempotency reference and applies per-tenant timeouts and circuit breakers.
  3. 03 Collect. The call goes to the MTN or Airtel Open API. Money-moving POSTs are never retried blindly.
  4. 04 Confirm. The provider's callback is written to an outbox and published to Kafka, so it survives a broker outage.
  5. 05 Settle. The PostgreSQL ledger is updated; reconciliation catches anything without a final status.

Every hop is traced, measured and alerted on in Grafana.
